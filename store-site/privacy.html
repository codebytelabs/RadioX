Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026 · Product: RadioX Chrome Extension · Publisher: Codebyte Labs

Overview

RadioX is an internet radio player for Chromium browsers. We designed it to be private by default: no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no account required to listen.

Information we collect

RadioX does not collect personal information on Codebyte Labs servers. The extension stores the following data locally in your browser using Chrome storage APIs:

  • Favorite stations and recently played stations
  • Playback preferences (volume, equalizer, sleep timer)
  • Optional track-log history you choose to keep
  • Optional supporter unlock flag (honor-system tip code)

This data stays on your device unless you export it yourself (for example, CSV export).

Network requests

To discover and play stations, RadioX contacts:

  • Radio Browser API (*.radio-browser.info) — station directory metadata
  • Station stream hosts — direct audio streams chosen by you when you press play. Because stations are hosted worldwide, the extension requests broad host access solely to connect to those streams.
  • Optional tip pages — if you open Buy Me a Coffee or Ko-fi support links, those sites process payments under their own privacy policies

RadioX does not sell your data and does not use third-party advertising or tracking pixels.

Permissions explained

  • storage — save favorites and settings on your device
  • offscreen / alarms / notifications — background playback and optional now-playing alerts
  • host access — fetch station directories and play HTTP/HTTPS radio streams

Children

RadioX is a general-audience listening tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
